Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction and Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used and protected in connection with the website dfexposure.ie (the "Website"). The Website is a personal portfolio, operated by me in my capacity as an individual and not through a registered company or other legal entity.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018, I, am the data controller in respect of personal data processed through the Website. I may be contacted at the email: dev@dfexposure.ie.

2. Personal Data Collected

I collect only the personal data necessary to operate the Website and to respond to enquiries. This comprises the following:

  • Information you provide. Where you contact me by means of the form on the Website, or by email, I receive your name, your email address, the content of your message, and any further information you elect to provide.
  • Technical information. The Website is hosted by GitHub Pages. In the course of delivering the Website to your device, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details. I do not have access to this data. GitHub processes it in accordance with its own privacy statement.

The Website does not use cookies or analytics tools.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed solely for the purpose of reading and responding to your communication, and for discussing any work or collaboration that you have enquired about. The legal bases relied upon are:

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where processing is necessary in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into an agreement; and
  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, namely my legitimate interest in responding to communications addressed to me.

Your personal data is not used for direct marketing, automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Disclosure of Personal Data

Personal data is not sold, rented or otherwise disclosed to third parties for their own purposes. Your communication may be handled by the following service providers, solely in order to operate the Website and to receive messages:

  • Formspree, as the query submission handler
  • Zoho Mail, as the email service provider
  • GitHub, as the host of the Website

Certain of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, the relevant provider is responsible for ensuring that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection law.

5. Retention

Enquiries are retained for 12 months following our last communication and are thereafter deleted. Where an engagement proceeds, I retain the correspondence and information necessary for the duration of that engagement and for any period required to satisfy legal or record-keeping obligations, including those relating to taxation.

6. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. The Website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage can, however, be guaranteed to be entirely secure.

7. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions set out in data protection law, you have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data I hold about you;
  • request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request the erasure of your personal data;
  • request the restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • request the transfer of your data to you or to another controller, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at [your email address]. I will respond within one month of receipt of your request.

8. Amendments to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be amended from time to time, for example where the operation of the Website changes. Any amendment will be published on this page, and the effective date above will be updated accordingly.
